# FeziPay Terms — draft for legal review

## 1. Operator and scope
FeziPay is an independently operated service, not Telegram, a bank, or a deposit insurer. The legal operator, business address and required authorizations must be disclosed before financial activation. This draft does not establish a licensed financial service.

## 2. Eligibility
The proposed financial service is intended for verified Iranian adults aged at least 18, only where service is lawful. Nationality alone does not determine eligibility. Users must have legal capacity and act for themselves or disclose authorized representation; false identities are prohibited.

## 3. Identity and account security
We may request proportionate identity and source-of-funds evidence through an approved secure channel. Never provide seed phrases, private keys, Telegram login codes or banking passwords. Changes to payout identity or credentials may require re-verification and a disclosed security hold.

## 4. Orders and consent
Every funded service requires a unique order stating asset, network, destination, amount, applicable fees and expiry before acceptance. Viewing a wallet address does not create an accepted order. Users must receive an accessible copy of the terms version they accept; material changes do not silently rewrite completed orders.

## 5. Asset and network matching
The sender must check the exact asset, network, token contract when applicable, address and required memo or destination tag. Similar address formats do not prove cross-network compatibility. Wrong-network recovery may be impossible; we must still investigate errors caused by our own incorrect instructions and cannot exclude mandatory liability.

## 6. Deposit confirmation
A screenshot, a transaction hash or a browser callback is not proof of settled funds. Credit requires independent verification of chain, asset, recipient, amount, finality and unique attribution to the order. Duplicate submissions, reorganizations, underpayments and overpayments require documented reconciliation; no balance is created merely from a user claim.

## 7. Service fees
The proposed base service fee is 15% of the expressly quoted eligible gross amount. Network costs and any conversion spread must be itemized separately before acceptance. A 100-unit example with a 15-unit service fee yields 85 units before disclosed network costs; the website calculator is an estimate, not a binding exchange quote.

## 8. Withdrawal and settlement clock
The proposed target is settlement within 72 hours after funds are actually received and confirmed, required identity checks are complete, and a valid withdrawal is accepted. This excludes Telegram’s own release or withdrawal waiting period. Official holidays, documented network outages or exceptional disruptions may delay processing; ordinary working days alone are not an exception. Provide a reason, revised estimate and complaint route.

## 9. Payout controls
Withdrawals must reserve the balance atomically so concurrent requests cannot spend the same funds twice. Payout address changes require renewed confirmation. A payout is completed only after independent on-chain confirmation and a receipt. Rial or toman settlement remains unavailable until a lawful banking route, same-name account checks and pricing are in place.

## 10. Telegram services
Creator rewards and personally purchased Stars are distinct and subject to Telegram’s current terms. We do not promise to cash out non-withdrawable balances or bypass Telegram restrictions. Digital goods and services sold inside Telegram bots and mini apps must use Telegram Stars where required; an external crypto website does not remove that requirement.

## 11. Merchant widget and API
A wallet-display widget only displays public payment details and does not verify, insure or settle payments. Merchants must clearly identify the actual recipient and separately agree refund and delivery obligations. Secret API credentials must stay on the server. Signed event verification, replay protection and independent chain checks are necessary before automated crediting.

## 12. Prohibited activity
Fraud, extortion, theft, phishing, impersonation, money laundering, unlawful fundraising and exploitation are prohibited. Users must not submit forged evidence, conceal beneficial ownership or route funds to evade applicable restrictions. We may decline orders that cannot be lawfully or reliably serviced without asserting criminal guilt merely from an automated flag.

## 13. Review, holds and appeals
A risk review may temporarily restrict a transaction where justified and permitted. Record the reason, scope, review deadline and appeal channel, subject to lawful confidentiality requirements. Suspicion alone does not grant us ownership of a customer’s money. Any continued hold, return or legally required freeze must have a documented basis; do not destroy relevant records to avoid accountability.

## 14. Refunds and mistakes
Refund eligibility, any genuinely incurred network costs and expected timing must be stated before an order. No blanket no-refund rule overrides mandatory rights or our own errors. Reimbursements go to a verified appropriate destination after ownership checks. Blockchain transfers may be irreversible, but that does not eliminate a contractual duty to correct an error when applicable.

## 15. Records and notices
Each customer should see only their own gross receipts, service fees, network costs, reserved amounts, payouts and order status. Administrator alerts must not expose identity documents, full secrets or private information in public groups. Transactional notifications are separate from optional marketing; promotional subscriptions can be withdrawn.

## 16. Risk and responsibility
Crypto prices, network fees, congestion, wallet compromise and third-party outages can cause losses. There is no investment return or uninterrupted-service guarantee. Users remain responsible for their unlawful conduct, while the operator remains responsible where applicable law or its own acts impose responsibility. Liability exclusions cannot erase fraud, non-excludable duties or mandatory consumer rights.

The legal operator, competent dispute forum and applicable law must be finalized by qualified counsel before activation; this draft does not invent a jurisdiction or remove access to mandatory remedies.

## 18. Changes, language and closure
English is the proposed reference language and Persian is an available translation; mandatory local protections prevail. Other language versions must not be represented as reviewed until they exist and are checked. Give notice of material changes and preserve accepted versions. Closing an account does not extinguish unresolved lawful payment obligations, complaints or required record retention.
