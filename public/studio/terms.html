Community & Content Rules

FEZI Studio includes AI model directory entries, self-hosted builders and community submissions. Published media are subject to moderation and lawful rights.

Accounts & usage

Keep your account secure. You may publish only work you own or are authorized to share. Account signup does not confirm email ownership or identity at this stage.

Consent & likeness

Non-consensual sexual deepfakes, intimate impersonations of real people, sexual content involving minors, harassment and deceptive impersonation are prohibited. The platform may support lawful non-explicit creative transformations with necessary rights and permissions.

Adult-oriented catalog

Some user-supplied model lists include adult-oriented community entries. An age checkbox is an acknowledgement, not reliable age verification. Age-restricted outputs are not enabled until appropriate verification, filtering, region restrictions and provider rules are implemented.

Public submissions

Saved projects and uploads are private by default. Publishing is opt-in and requires owner approval; downloads and prompt remixes follow creator permissions. You can request removal and dispute moderation decisions through the operator.

Payments

Wallet addresses are not payment confirmation. Balance credits and premium access are disabled pending verified processor integration. Verify token, network and address before transfers.

AI transparency

Arina is an AI avatar, not a human streamer. The technology provider and internal model weights need not be disclosed in a public interface, but the AI nature of the character must not be hidden.
